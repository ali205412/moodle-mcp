# Plan 09-02: Gradebook And Badge Parity Wrappers

## Intent

Close the remaining gradebook setup and badge administration gaps with typed wrappers over Moodle’s existing grade-item, grade-category, and badge model APIs.

## Deliverables

1. Wrapper definitions and execution helpers for manual grade-item CRUD/move and grade-category update/delete/move.
2. Wrapper definitions and execution helpers for badge create/update/message/delete/clone, related-badge, alignment, and manual award/revoke flows.
3. PHPUnit coverage for representative gradebook and badge wrapper flows.
